COMPILERLAB - ONE-CLICK START GUIDE (Windows + VS Code)

1. Extract the entire ZIP folder first (Right-click > Extract All).
2. Double-click START_APP.bat inside "CD CAP"
   - Automatically installs Python & npm packages (first run),
   - Starts Flask Backend (port 5000) & Vite Frontend (port 5173),
   - Opens http://localhost:5173 in your browser.

Alternative (Inside VS Code):
- Open the "CD CAP" folder in VS Code, open "run.py", and click the Play/Run button (top-right).
